continuous_oblique_v061.py

Purpose
-------
v0.6.1 corrects the v0.6 membership logic. Span visibility is QA metadata only.
A legal continuous-oblique image is kept even when it shows only part of a span,
one structure group, or no structure group. Images are excluded only by the
existing sequence-membership rules such as crossing/side/additional-flight/outside-AOI.

Smoke test
----------
python .\continuous_oblique_v061.py `
  "D:\Sev\Obliques\NYPA" `
  --circuit DH1 `
  --render-limit 10 `
  --output "D:\Sev\Obliques\NYPA\_continuous_oblique_poc_v061_smoke"

Expected behaviour on the previously reviewed first 10 frames
--------------------------------------------------------------
- all 10 legal sequence frames remain in 163_candidate
- span-visibility QA should classify roughly 6 FULL and 4 PARTIAL
- the four previously called FAIL are now kept, not removed
- qa\span_completeness uses full_span and partial_span folders
- continuous-product coverage should equal legal sequence coverage, not the old
  span-complete-only 67.92% figure

Important fields
----------------
sequence_keep             Product membership. This controls whether the image stays.
valid_for_163              Compatibility alias for sequence_keep in v0.6.1.
reference_id               Label written to the image.
reference_confidence       Confidence in that label.
alternative_reference      Adjacent reference when boundary-ambiguous.
span_visibility_class      FULL_SPAN / PARTIAL_SPAN / SINGLE_STRUCTURE /
                           NO_STRUCTURE_VISIBLE / NOT_APPLICABLE.
full_span_visible          QA boolean only. Never used to remove a legal frame.
span_view_status           Detailed geometry evidence status.

Notes
-----
Do not use span visibility to prune the sequence. A partial-frame bridge may be
exactly what makes the product continuous. DINO/visual continuity can be added in
the next phase to evaluate adjacent legal frames without deleting them automatically.
