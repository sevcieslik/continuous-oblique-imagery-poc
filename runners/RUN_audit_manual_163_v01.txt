  torch 2.6.0+cpu
and can load:
  facebook/dinov3-vits16-pretrain-lvd1689m

Default DINO source mode
------------------------
raw-when-trusted:
- HIGH/MEDIUM manual-final -> raw matches use the original raw image for DINO,
  avoiding lower-right delivery annotation bias.
- unresolved/LOW matches use the manual final JPG itself.

To force DINO to inspect only delivered manual JPG pixels:
  --feature-source manual
