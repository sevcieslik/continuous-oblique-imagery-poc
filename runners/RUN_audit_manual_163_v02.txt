What changed
------------
1. Very high DINO similarity between local consecutive/nearby frames is treated as
   EXPECTED_HIGH_OVERLAP, not as a duplicate error.
2. POSSIBLE_REDUNDANT_FRAME is a mild Grade-B review signal.
3. TRUE_DUPLICATE_SUSPECT is reserved for same-raw reuse or extreme similarity far
   apart in run/span/chainage space.
4. Numerical missing-span checks are validated against reference_spans.csv.
5. Calibration trust is graded A / B / REVIEW.
6. The script automatically reuses the v0.1 DINO cache when
   D:\Sev\Obliques\DH1\_audit_manual_163_v01\qa\visual_continuity\feature_cache exists.

Calibration grades
------------------
A       High/medium raw match + normal pass + auditable geometry + no material flags.
B       Independently auditable, but with only mild QA flags.
REVIEW  Source mapping uncertain, geometry insufficient, wrong-pass/sequence/label
        concern, or strong duplicate suspicion.

No image is deleted or relabelled automatically.
