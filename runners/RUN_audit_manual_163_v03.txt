Manual 163 audit v0.3

Why v0.3
--------
v0.2 showed 95 TRUE_DUPLICATE_SUSPECT pairs, but inspection of duplicate_candidates.csv
showed that all 95 were being triggered by the same matched_raw_filename. That is not
sufficient evidence when either manual-final -> raw match is LOW/unresolved.

v0.3 changes the logic:
- same raw filename only counts as literal reuse when BOTH source matches are HIGH/MEDIUM;
- LOW/unresolved same-raw collisions become RAW_MAPPING_COLLISION_UNTRUSTED and do not
  reduce calibration trust;
- HIGH/MEDIUM same-raw reuse in a local sequence becomes POSSIBLE_REDUNDANT_FRAME (Grade B),
  not a strong duplicate error;
- HIGH/MEDIUM same-raw reuse far apart in span/run space remains TRUE_DUPLICATE_SUSPECT;
- different raw frames can still become TRUE_DUPLICATE_SUSPECT when DINO itself is
  extremely similar far apart;
- local high DINO similarity is learned relative to the product and reported as
  EXPECTED_HIGH_OVERLAP. This is neutral evidence.

The 9 missing manual spans remain checked against reference topology.
